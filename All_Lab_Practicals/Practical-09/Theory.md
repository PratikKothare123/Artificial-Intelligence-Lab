# Practical 09 – Bayes Rule for Rain Prediction

## Aim
To evaluate Bayes Rule for rain prediction from a given scenario.

---

## Introduction
Bayes Theorem is a fundamental concept in probability theory used to calculate 
conditional probability. It helps in updating the probability of an event based 
on new evidence.

Bayes Theorem formula:

P(A|B) = [P(B|A) × P(A)] / P(B)

Where:
- P(A|B) → Probability of event A given B
- P(B|A) → Probability of event B given A
- P(A) → Prior probability of A
- P(B) → Total probability of B

---

## Concept
In this practical, Bayes theorem is used to predict whether it will rain based 
on given conditions such as cloud presence or humidity.

---

## Working Principle
1. Define prior probability of rain.
2. Define probability of evidence (e.g., clouds).
3. Apply Bayes formula.
4. Calculate posterior probability.
5. Interpret the result.

---

## Example Scenario
- Probability of rain = 0.3
- Probability of clouds given rain = 0.8
- Probability of clouds = 0.5

- Used in prediction systems

Bayes theorem provides a powerful method for prediction based on probability.
