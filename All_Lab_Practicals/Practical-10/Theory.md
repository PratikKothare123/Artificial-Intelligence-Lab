# Practical 10 – Prompt Engineering for Adversarial Search

## Aim
To apply prompt engineering techniques to construct an effective prompt for learning the concept of Adversarial Search.

---

## Introduction
Prompt Engineering is the process of designing and structuring inputs (prompts) 
to guide AI models in generating accurate and meaningful responses.

It plays an important role in Artificial Intelligence, especially when interacting 
with language models like ChatGPT.

---

## Adversarial Search
Adversarial Search is used in Artificial Intelligence for decision-making in 
multi-agent environments where agents compete against each other.

Example:
- Chess
- Tic Tac Toe
- Checkers

In such problems:
- One player tries to maximize the score (MAX)
- Other player tries to minimize the score (MIN)

---

## Concept of Prompt Engineering
A good prompt should be:
- Clear and specific
- Structured properly
- Provide context
- Ask for expected output

---

## Constructed Prompt
"Explain the concept of Adversarial Search in Artificial Intelligence with examples. 
Include explanation of Minimax algorithm and Alpha-Beta Pruning in simple language. 
Also provide advantages and applications."

---

## Working Principle
1. Identify the topic (Adversarial Search)
2. Define what information is needed
3. Construct a clear and detailed prompt
4. Generate response using AI model

---

## Advantages
- Improves AI output quality
- Saves time
- Provides accurate results
- Useful in learning and development

---

## Applications
- Chatbots
- AI learning systems
- Content generation
- Programming assistance

---

## Conclusion
Prompt engineering helps in generating better responses from AI systems. 
By designing effective prompts, users can easily learn complex topics like 
Adversarial Search in a simple way.
